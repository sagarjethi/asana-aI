The problem

Beautiful to do. Impossible to judge. Hard to watch.

  • The discipline that makes yoga stunning — millimetric alignment, held stillness, control — is invisible to a camera audience.
  • Human judges disagree on subtle deviations. Contested deductions can't be defended on air.
  • A viewer can't see why one athlete beats another. No "why" = no fans = no sport.
Why now

The category is ready — the technology just arrived.

  • Yoga is global and mainstream — hundreds of millions practise; a built-in audience and talent pool.
  • Sport-tech precedent is proven — Hawk-Eye, DRS, Simulcam taught fans to trust machine-assisted officiating.
  • Markerless 3D pose is finally good enough — multi-camera capture now resolves real joint angles, camera-only.
  • We already shipped the hard part — AsanaAI runs real-time pose tech in production today.

The solution

One platform. Two layers. Total transparency.

Layer A

Scoring Support

Objective, camera-only measurement that assists referees: joint-angle deviation, stability, hold time, candidate deductions — every one explainable and replayable.

Layer B

Broadcast & Fans

Real-time AR graphics, live scores, leaderboards, Simulcam ghost comparisons, and a second-screen app that makes the sport legible and gripping.

The principle

Augment, never replace

The machine suggests; the human judge confirms. Every score is signed, auditable, and survives a televised protest.

How it works

Multi-camera 3D — and two tiers, like DRS.

  • 6–8 synced cameras triangulate a true 3D skeleton in millimetre space — no wearables.
  • Live tier (~60–90 ms) drives on-air graphics and the "feel".
  • Adjudicated tier computes the official, replayable, signed deduction in a short review.
The trust backbone

AI suggests. The judge confirms.

The referee console shows measured joint angles, candidate deductions, and a confidence for each — Approve / Override one tap away.

  • Low confidence → the system abstains and defers to the human.
  • Every override is logged. Every score is reproducible.
  • This is the single feature that earns a federation's trust.
Broadcast experience

A sport that explains itself on screen.

  • Alignment & angle indicators, centre-of-gravity markers, live score breakdowns.
  • Leaderboards and round summaries that keep viewers oriented.
  • Graphics keyed straight into the live feed — broadcast-grade.
The wow moment

"Show me why."

Tap any score and the replay shows the exact joint, the exact angle, and the exact reason: "standing knee flexed 8° — alignment −0.3."

Black-box scoring is rejectable. Evidence is undeniable. This turns disputes into highlights.

Fan engagement

From passive viewer to active judge.

A second-screen app turns watching into playing — and teaches scoring literacy that grows the sport.

  • Judge-along — score the athlete, compare to the AI and the real judges.
  • Live win-probability and prediction games.
  • Highlights and shareable clips, auto-generated from the score stream.

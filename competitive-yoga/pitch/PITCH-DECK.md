## How this deck is built (storytelling principles)
1. **One headline = one idea** — each slide's title lands in a 3-second glance; read only the
   headlines and you still get the whole pitch.
2. **A narrative arc, not a feature dump** — *World → Problem → Why now → Solution → How → Trust →
   Wow → Broadcast → Product walkthrough → Who it helps → Moat → Market → Proof → Plan → Ask.*
3. **One visual theme** — warm "sunrise" palette, one emphasized image per slide.

## The "who it helps" slide (end-to-end value)
- **🧘 Athletes — from living-room to the podium:** free practice app with real-time feedback, AI
  training plans & weakness drills, progress tracking, ranking/scouting, a path amateur → competitor → pro.
- **🎯 Coaches — develop talent with data:** weakness heatmaps, score trends, head-to-head &
  benchmarking, pre/post-match review, drill plans.
- **⚖️ Judges — officiate with confidence:** evidence-backed explainable calls, faster consistent
  scoring, defensible under protest, train & certify junior judges.
- *Closing line:* one account, one journey — the same platform that scores a world final helps a
  beginner improve at home.

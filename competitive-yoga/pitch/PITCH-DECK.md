## Market data on slides 11–12 (verified by research QA)
- **TAM $127B** — global yoga market (Grand View Research), ~10% CAGR → **$269B by 2033**.
- **SAM ~$6B** — digital & competitive yoga (online/app/virtual + officiating-tech wedge).
- **SOM $25–35M ARR** by Year 5 — bottom-up from unit economics, **<1% of SAM**.
- **~300M** practise yoga worldwide (est.); **$106B** virtual-fitness tailwind by 2030 (~28% CAGR).
- Precedents: **Tennis** — Hawk-Eye replaced all line judges at Wimbledon 2025; **Gymnastics** — FIG adopted Fujitsu AI/LiDAR judging; **Esports** — $2.5B→$7.5B, 611M fans.
- *TAM deliberately anchored on the single yoga figure (no double-counting of overlapping B2B/B2C pools); broadcasting/media-rights are framed as tailwinds, not addressable market.*
